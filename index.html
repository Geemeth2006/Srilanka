Popular Cities

Badulla

Badulla is a beautiful city in Sri Lanka's Uva Province, surrounded by lush mountains, waterfalls, and tea plantations. It is known for its cool climate, rich history, scenic railway, and peaceful natural beauty.

Nuwara Eliya

Nuwara Eliya is a picturesque hill city in central Sri Lanka, famous for its cool climate, lush tea plantations, beautiful gardens, waterfalls, and colonial architecture. It is often called "Little England" because of its British influence.

Matara

Matara is a coastal city in southern Sri Lanka, known for its beautiful beaches, historic fort, and vibrant local culture. It offers a mix of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and a laid-back atmosphere for visitors.

Anuradhapura

Anuradhapura is an ancient city in northern Sri Lanka, once the capital of the island's earliest kingdoms. It is home to numerous historical sites, including the Ruwanwelisaya Stupa and the Abhayagiri Vihara, showcasing the rich Buddhist heritage and architectural legacy of the region.
