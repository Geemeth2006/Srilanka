Popular Cities

Badulla

Badulla is a beautiful city in Sri Lanka's Uva Province, surrounded by lush mountains, waterfalls, and tea plantations. It is known for its cool climate, rich history, scenic railway, and peaceful natural beauty.

Nuwara Eliya

Nuwara Eliya is a picturesque hill city in central Sri Lanka, famous for its cool climate, lush tea plantations, beautiful gardens, waterfalls, and colonial architecture. It is often called "Little England" because of its British influence.

Matara

Matara is a vibrant coastal city in southern Sri Lanka, known for its beautiful beaches, historic landmarks, and rich cultural heritage. It features the Dutch Fort, Weherahena Temple, and a thriving fishing and tourism industry.

Anuradhapura

Anuradhapura is an ancient city in Sri Lanka, famous for its sacred Buddhist temples, ancient ruins, and historic monuments. It is home to the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
